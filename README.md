# NEON SNAKE 🐍
## A Classic Arcade Challenge, Reimagined

Guide a glowing snake through a vibrant neon arena in NEON SNAKE, a

modern take on the timeless mobile-phone classic. Collect food, grow

longer, beat your high score, and stay one step ahead of the obstacles

standing in your way.

EAT, GROW, AND SURVIVE Every piece of food earns points and makes your

snake longer. The arena becomes more challenging as obstacles appear, so

plan your turns carefully. Avoid crashing into walls, your own tail, or

solid obstacles—or use a power-up to change the odds.

DISCOVER POWER-UPS Special items can appear around the arena, each with

its own chance of spawning and a temporary effect:

- Shield: Protects the snake from collisions for a limited time.

- Ice Orb: Temporarily boosts the snake’s movement speed.

- Laser Beam: Fires in the direction of travel and destroys obstacles

in its path.

Power-ups add a tactical twist to every run. Decide when to collect them

and make the most of their short-lived abilities.

MAKE IT YOUR RUN Play using keyboard controls, touch-screen direction

buttons, or swipe gestures. Switch between English and Russian, pause

whenever you need a break, and restart a run at any time. The responsive

interface is designed to work on both desktop and mobile screens.

CHASE YOUR PERSONAL BEST Your score and play statistics are saved

locally in your browser. The game uses AES-GCM encryption through the

Web Crypto API for its saved data. No account is required.

BUILT TO BE EXTENDED NEON SNAKE includes a simple JavaScript bonus

registry, making it easy to add custom collectible items. Developers can

define a bonus’s icon, name, description, spawn chance, duration, and

custom behavior. Registered items are automatically listed in the

in-game Help section.

HOW TO PLAY 1. Move around the arena and collect the glowing food. 2.

Avoid the walls, your own body, and obstacles. 3. Collect power-ups to

gain temporary advantages. 4. Keep growing, earn points, and try to beat

your best score.

One arena. Endless turns. How long can you survive?

Note: Saved-game encryption is intended to make stored data less

readable, not to provide tamper-proof anti-cheat protection. The

encryption key is included in the client-side game code.

Warning: artificial intelligence technologies are used in the development of the game.
